This time we have a special Social! In cooperation with the young ADFC Rhein-Neckar e.V. and the Stadtjugendring e.V., we will show you the open-air film *“Wadjda”* on 12.09. The film tells the story of a 10-year-old girl in Saudi Arabia who dreams of owning a bicycle – even though this is actually not intended for girls in her society. With courage and resourcefulness, Wadjda tries to make her wish come true.
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

Something special: The open-air cinema is powered by eight bike trainers that generate the electricity for the sound system and the screen. So throughout the entire film it’s all about: pedal, pedal, pedal!🚲🦿
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

Location: Stadtjugendring grounds  

Harbigweg 5, 69124 Heidelberg
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

Admission: 19:00  

Start: 20:00
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

Please bring picnic blankets or camping chairs to sit on. In case of bad weather, we will find an alternative location. Snacks and drinks to share are also very welcome.🎥🍿
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

Admission is free. If you like, you can support with a donation of any amount – as a guideline, think of the price of a road bike tube🛞. We will send out the donation link soon, and there will also be a donation box on site. It’s especially cool if you support the young ADFC Heidelberg in its traffic education work.🫶🏼🦖
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

Spread the word – everyone is invited!
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

We look forward to seeing you there!🥳
