Cycling Dinner: Registration open!!🦖🍛🚴🏼‍♀️🚴‍♂️ —> on 18.04.26

<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

After the last Cycling Dinner was so well received and many of you asked for another one, we thought we would repeat it at the start of the summer semester!🌸☀️

<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

At the Cycling Dinner, two people always form a team that prepares one course (starter, main course, or dessert) of the three course menu. The teams are formed randomly, but you can also register as a pair. You will eat the dish you prepared with two other teams at your place, and for the other two courses you will cycle to a new location and meet different teams there. This way you can get into conversations, meet many new 🦖, try new food, and explore the city!

<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>
