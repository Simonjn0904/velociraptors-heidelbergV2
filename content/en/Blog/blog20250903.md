Do you like cake 🎂? Do you like cycling 🚲? Best of all at the same time? Then welcome to Loops & Cake 🧁!  
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

On 09/28 we’ll spend a whole day in the Odenwald—riding loops and eating loads of cake. In the morning we’ll head together to our basepoint—the Asta-Hütte above Heiligkreuzsteinach 🛖. There, we’ll set up a cozy spot with blankets 🧣 and hot drinks 🫖. From there, we’ve planned 5 different loops, and you can ride as many as you want—or can manage. The genius part: each loop leads past another hut where you’ll be rewarded with a delicious piece of cake 🍰 for your effort. Plus, at lunchtime we’ll order pizza 🍕 together to make the event a true culinary-cardiovascular spectacle.  

Sounds good? Then mark 09/28 in your calendar, RSVP so we know how much cake to bake, and tell your friends so we can fill the Odenwald with cyclists and cake lovers!  
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>
