13.09.2025  
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

Thanks to everyone who joined us yesterday at the Bike Cinema with the film *Wadjda*! Swipe to see a picture of the evening 😍  
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

Since it was such a success, we’re doing it again – so: Save the Date, October 2!  
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

In connection with the fall of the Berlin Wall, we’ll be watching the film *Zwei zu Eins*, in which three friends stumble upon a GDR vault full of money and must decide how to handle this treasure in a completely transformed society.  
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

The open-air cinema will be powered by eight bike trainers that generate electricity for the sound system and screen. For most, that means leaning back and relaxing – but for a few volunteers: pedal, pedal, pedal! 🚲🦿  
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

**Location:** Stadtjugendring grounds  

Harbigweg 5, 69124 Heidelberg  
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

**Doors open:** 7:00 PM  

**Start:** 8:00 PM  
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

Please bring picnic blankets or camping chairs to sit on. In case of bad or very cold weather, we’ll move indoors. Snacks and drinks to share are also very welcome. 🎥🍿  
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

Admission is free. We’re especially happy if people are interested in getting involved with the young ADFC Heidelberg and helping advance traffic education work. 🫶🏼🦖  
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

Spread the word and bring your friends – everyone is warmly invited!  
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

We’re looking forward to seeing you there! 🥳  
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>
