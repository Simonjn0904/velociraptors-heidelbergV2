<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

An eventful year is drawing to a close, and we can look back with great pride on the successes and challenges of the Dino year 2025.
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

**Highlights**
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

After nights of HTML coding and countless error messages, our website finally went live in spring. An important step for the community and a project that significantly increased our visibility. In August, our Cycling Camp at Lake Maggiore followed. Fantastic weather, 24 participants, and a week that will stay in our minds for a long time. Throughout the year, there were numerous fast rides and social rides, sometimes with more than 40 people on the road. In autumn, things became cozier with Loops and Cakes, this time with more than 50 participants. The FLINTA and queer group was founded and has been developing very positively ever since. This was complemented by many off bike socials, where it became clear that this community is far more than just riding bikes together. And last but not least, our Dino merch launched, above all the socks, which became surprisingly popular very quickly. You can still order last minute Christmas gifts [*here*](/de/explore/merch/).
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

**New Structures and Perspectives**
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

2025 was shaped by strong growth. A growth that makes us proud, grateful, and sometimes also a little speechless. In the WhatsApp community, we now count nearly 700 people, on Instagram more than 900. Individual posts have reached well over 10,000 views. This shows that we radiate an open and welcoming culture. At the same time, this very fact makes organization demanding and at times has overwhelmed us.
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

On important structural questions, there have been and still are significant differences of opinion. Topics such as dealing with discrimination, macho behavior in WhatsApp groups or during group rides, and our identity as a community overall were discussed intensively. Meetings with more than 25 participants were not always easy, but they showed enormous commitment and genuine passion. In the end, we emerged stronger and were able to find viable compromises. As a result, a community guideline was initiated, and we are currently evaluating our census together. The current status can be viewed in the [*Documents*](/de/explore/documents/) section and is not yet finalized.
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

**Looking Ahead to 2026**
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

Looking toward 2026, we are already planning another Cycling Camp and developing new events. Many existing formats will of course remain. At the same time, we want to continue addressing organizational questions. This includes, for example, how we can make participation more attractive and hear your opinions earlier. The awareness concept will also be further developed. We look forward to every new face at rides, socials, and meetings.
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

**A Personal Christmas Wish for the Community**
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

For 2026, I particularly wish to further advance the work of the diversity team. In an increasingly polarized society, it would be powerful if our community actively showed that inclusion, tolerance, and solidarity have a future. Social projects such as joint rides with refugees, open film evenings with the bicycle cinema, or cooperation with NGOs that clearly oppose the societal shift to the right can set visible signs. Or to quote German rapper Disarstar: stay strong even when everything is shaking. Help with the website and writing articles are of course also very welcome.
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

**Thanks and Conclusion**
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

A huge thank you goes to everyone who supports us at events and helps ensure that this community thrives with so many wonderful people. None of this would be possible without the many helping hands, whether leading group rides or working behind the scenes. This year, the organization was especially carried by Zak, Sebastian, Moritz, Teddy, Lydia, Daniel, Jonna, Lynn, Jannik, Cosi, Linda, Ida, Ren, Tom, Berkay, Jakob, Hanno, Clara, Fabian, and Oriana. If I have forgotten anyone, please forgive me and feel free to reach out to me 🫶🏼.
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

All of us take immense joy in this.
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

Make yourselves comfortable, enjoy quiet days on the couch, and Merry Christmas to everyone who celebrates.
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

With lots of love

Simon
