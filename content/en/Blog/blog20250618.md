We would like to introduce a FLINTA* (i.e. women, lesbians, intersex, non-binary, trans and agender) Group in our -WhatsApp-Community. The goal is to create a safe space where we can continue to meet casually for rides and exchange ideas, with room for questions, comments, and criticism; anything that might feel difficult or intimidating in 'General'. 🦖
Anyone who is interested and feels like they might belong here is welcome to join the group. It's important to us that everyone feels safe and comfortable here, whether meeting new people or riding in the group for the first time... 🦕✨
