Dinosaurs are unfortunately extinct. Or maybe not entirely? The most recently dated Velociraptor lived around 70 million years ago. In theory, it could have kept roaming the landscape for some time after that, just without the courtesy of leaving behind fossils. Four million years later, the great cosmic party pooper arrived. An asteroid struck Earth and wiped out around 75 percent of all species. All non avian dinosaurs were history for good.

But we are bringing it back. Not genetically, but in style. You placed a Velociraptor sticker somewhere in the world? Then help us repopulate the planet. Enter the location in the form and we will add your raptor to the interactive world map. Evolution 2.0, this time with stickers.
