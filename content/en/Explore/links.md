**ADFC Rhein-Neckar ([Link](https://rhein-neckar.adfc.de))**

ADFC Heidelberg is the local branch of the Allgemeiner Deutscher Fahrrad-Club (General German Bicycle Club). In this region, they advocate for better cycling conditions, safer infrastructure, and a sustainable mobility transition. In addition to political talks, cycling demos, and actions, they organize events for everyone who enjoys riding bikes.  
The young ADFC Heidelberg – their youth group – is currently looking for people interested in supporting traffic education. Specifically, it’s about teaching children and young people safe and confident cycling in a playful way. The work is paid. **[Here](https://rhein-neckar.adfc.de/verkehrspaedagogik)** you can find more info. If you’d like to get involved, feel free to reach out!

**Stadtjugendring Heidelberg ([Link](https://www.sjr-heidelberg.de/))** 

The Stadtjugendring Heidelberg is an umbrella organization of youth associations, clubs, and initiatives in Heidelberg that represents the interests of young people in the city. It promotes child and youth work, supports member organizations both organizationally and financially, and offers platforms for exchange, networking, and cooperation. In addition, the Stadtjugendring represents the concerns of young people to politics, administration, and the public, organizes its own projects and events, and thus actively contributes to strengthening youth participation, democratic engagement, and social diversity in Heidelberg.

**Stop Bezahlkarte Heidelberg ([Link](https://bezahlkarte-stoppen.de/de/))**

Not related to cycling, but surely interesting for all solidarity-minded Dinos! “Stop Bezahlkarte Heidelberg” is an alliance that opposes the introduction of the payment card for refugees in Heidelberg. To give refugees more freedom of action despite these restrictions, the initiative organizes an exchange market: refugees bring vouchers worth 50 euros, which they have received through the payment card, and supportive allies buy these vouchers. In this way, the credit is converted back into cash, giving those affected more freedom to manage their money while also sending a signal of solidarity.

**No Step to the Right ([Link](https://keinschrittnachrechts.de/))**

No Step to the Right is a civil society alliance in Heidelberg that positions itself against right-wing extremism, racism, and discriminatory ideologies. It brings together different groups, organizations, and committed individuals to stand jointly for democracy, diversity, and solidarity. The alliance organizes demonstrations, rallies, and educational programs to expose right-wing structures, prevent their normalization, and strengthen counter-positions within the city’s society. Its goal is to send a clear public signal that there is no place in Heidelberg for right-wing agitation, exclusion, and violence.
