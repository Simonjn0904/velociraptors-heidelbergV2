**Junger ADFC Rhein-Neckar ([Link](https://rhein-neckar.adfc.de))**

ADFC Rhein-Neckar is the local branch of the Allgemeiner Deutscher Fahrrad-Club (General German Cyclists’ Association). They advocate for better cycling conditions in the region, safer infrastructure, and a sustainable transport transition. In addition to political dialogue, bike demos, and campaigns, they also organize events for everyone who enjoys cycling.  
Junger ADFC Rhein-Neckar, their youth group, is currently looking for people who would like to get involved in traffic education. Specifically, the goal is to teach children and teenagers safe and confident cycling in a playful way. This work is paid. You can find more information **[here](https://rhein-neckar.adfc.de/verkehrspaedagogik)**. If you would like to get involved, feel free to reach out!

**Stadtjugendring Heidelberg ([Link](https://www.sjr-heidelberg.de/))** 

Stadtjugendring Heidelberg is an association of youth organisations, clubs, and initiatives in Heidelberg. As an umbrella organisation, it represents the interests of young people in the city. It promotes child and youth work, supports member organisations both organisationally and financially, and offers platforms for exchange, networking, and cooperation. In addition, it represents young people’s concerns toward politics, administration, and the public, organises its own projects and events, and actively strengthens youth participation, democratic involvement, and social diversity in Heidelberg. With our club, for example, we attend the open-air bicycle cinema.

**Bezahlkarte stoppen Heidelberg ([Link](https://bezahlkarte-stoppen.de/de/))**

Not directly related to cycling, but definitely interesting for all solidarity-minded dinos! “Bezahlkarte stoppen Heidelberg” is an alliance campaigning against the introduction of the payment card for refugees in Heidelberg. To give refugees more room to act despite restrictions, the initiative organises an exchange scheme. Refugees bring vouchers worth 50 euros, for example Aldi or Rewe, which they receive via the payment card, and supportive people buy these vouchers from them. This converts the credit back into cash, giving affected people more freedom in how they use their money while also sending a clear sign of solidarity.
<div style="margin-top: 1.0rem;"></div>

**Kein Schritt nach Rechts ([Link](https://keinschrittnachrechts.de/))**

Kein Schritt nach Rechts is a civil society alliance in Heidelberg that takes a stand against right-wing extremism, racism, and discriminatory ideologies. It brings together different groups, organisations, and engaged individuals to jointly advocate for democracy, diversity, and a solidarity-based community. The alliance organises demonstrations, rallies, and educational events to make right-wing structures visible, prevent their normalisation, and strengthen opposing positions in the city’s public sphere. The goal is to send a clear public message that there is no place in Heidelberg for right-wing agitation, exclusion, and violence.

**Jugenhilfe Ostafrika e.V. ([Link](https://www.jugendhilfe-ostafrika.de/))** 

Jugendhilfe Ostafrika e.V. is a non-profit organisation that has supported projects in Uganda for over 30 years. Its focus areas are mobility, education, and climate protection. Bicycles are financed through sponsorships and provided to families, self-help groups, or people with disabilities to improve transport options, income opportunities, and access to education. The organisation also supports children’s projects in Jinja and Fort Portal. In addition, it reforests areas. A bicycle can be donated or co-financed for 90 euros.
